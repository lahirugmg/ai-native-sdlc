# SDLC Opportunity Map

Use this template to find a practical next step on your journey to AI-native SDLC. Map one real feature, change, incident, or operational request. The point is to identify useful work and the evidence it needs, not to create an idealized process diagram.

Use the following guide when choosing a horizon:

| Horizon | Select it when | Do not select it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short term | The task is low risk, bounded, reviewable, and primarily needs approved tools, practice, and a clear task brief. | Tool access, data boundaries, or normal review controls are unclear. |
| Medium term | The workflow needs trusted requirements, code, architecture, standards, policies, or operational facts that are currently hard to find or verify. | The source of truth, owner, freshness, or access boundary is unknown. |
| Long term | The grounded task is recurring and clear enough to define inputs, outputs, exceptions, evaluations, controls, support, and rollback. | The task cannot be tested, supported, or safely stopped when it fails. |
