# AI Task Brief

Use this template for a bounded task where AI assistance may be useful. It is a thinking and communication aid, not a replacement for requirements, design, review, or approval.

## Instructions and Boundaries

```text
Outcome:
```

State what must not be assumed, changed, exposed, or invented. For uncertain work, ask for questions, risks, and options before asking for a final recommendation.

## Verify Before Use

- [ ] Check claims against the cited or underlying sources.
- [ ] Review assumptions, omissions, and contradictions.
- [ ] Run relevant tests, linters, security checks, or operational validation.
- [ ] Apply normal peer review and approval requirements.
- [ ] Remove or correct unsupported content before sharing or acting on it.
- [ ] Record a useful pattern or a notable failure for the team when appropriate.
